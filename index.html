Steps
    How it works & the rules
    • 2D shapes: Circle, Square, Triangle.
    • 3D shapes (a pair of 2D shapes): Sphere = Circle+Circle, Cube = Square+Square, Pyramid = Triangle+Triangle, Cone = Circle+Triangle, Cylinder = Circle+Square, Prism = Square+Triangle.
    • Inside rule: each solo player must build the 3D shape made of the two shapes that are not their own. Practically: everyone sends their own shape to the other two players, so each ends holding the other two players' shapes.
    • Outside rule: each statue must end holding a 3D shape that does not contain its own inside shape (= the two other players' shapes). You change a statue by dissecting one 2D component off it and dunking it on another statue; two dissections at once = a component swap.
    • The solver searches for the fewest swaps (each swap = 2 dunks) and handles the 3-swap "rotation" case automatically.
