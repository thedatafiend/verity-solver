Optimal The three ideas that carry the whole raid

Every encounter page has an Optimal section with its specific line. These are the patterns underneath them.

1 · Fail the charge on purpose. In Substratum, Dissipation and Repository, bouncing the signal spawns Resonance and completing the circuit consumes it. Bounce a few times, then deliberately drop the catch. The circuit stays open, the Resonance stays on the ground, and you spawn one punishment miniboss at a moment you chose — buying a quiet window to stack to x3 in the right order before you go back and lock.

This is the single most valuable habit in Salvation's Edge. Learn it in Substratum where it's cheapest.

2 · Read everything before anyone acts. Conduit symbols, Verity's shape callout, the last hand destroyed at Zenith — every mechanic in this raid is cheap to execute and expensive to redo. One caller, one verifier, no bullets or footsteps until the read is agreed.

Almost every "we ran out of time" in this raid is really "we did it twice."

3 · Batch your punishments. Subjugators at Zenith don't stack while one is alive, so simultaneous glyph breaks skip whole spawn sets. Verity's two puzzles are independent, so run them at the same time. Wherever the encounter lets you do several things on one count, do them on one count.

Raid The basics

  • Where: The Pale Heart of the Traveler — climbing the Witness's monolith. Released June 7, 2024 with The Final Shape (ownership required).
  • Fireteam: 6 players. Every encounter builds on the central Resonance mechanic and its three shapes — triangle, square, circle.
  • Time budget: experienced teams clear in roughly 2–4 hours; learning teams should plan 6–12 hours, often split across sessions. This is widely considered the most mechanically demanding raid in the game — communication is the real power level.
  • Power: the power system was reworked in 2025; be at or near the current seasonal caps and check in-game recommendations.

Extras Secret chests & the red-border puzzle

  • Chest 1 — near the end of the Monolith jumping puzzle (between Dissipation and Repository): at the amber-slab wall guarded by Dread, go right to the right-most amber cluster and slide under the gap in the wall; the chest is across that room.
  • Chest 2 — in the final climb before the Witness, in the area circling the giant spiraling root just before the boss arena.
  • Bonus red-border chest: a raid-long conductor puzzle keyed to the Resonance shapes shown in the raid's first room. Three hidden conductor rooms are active per run — charge each active conductor with its matching Resonance and a chest with a guaranteed Deepsight raid weapon appears after the Witness dies.

Prep Loadout notes

  • Composition: one healing/support super (Well of Radiance still earns its slot), one debuff (Divinity or Tractor Cannon), everyone else on burst DPS.
  • Weapons: strong add-clear everywhere — the whole raid throws Dread at you while you solve puzzles. Bring a long-range precision option (Substratum and Zenith mechanics) and swap-style rockets, heavy burst, or swords for boss phases. The Witness allows close-range play, so sword strategies are viable; Euphony itself is strong there once you have it.
  • Champions appear on Master — bring stuns to match the active mods.
  • Specific god rolls shift with the sandbox — treat this as archetype advice and check current community resources before a serious attempt.
