Encounter 3

Repository

Three floors, same loop, twistier wiring every time. No boss — just execution.

TL;DR The whole encounter in eight lines

  1. Three duos — Left, Middle, Right. Each owns its plate pair, Tormentor, and conduit.
  2. Dunk the free Resonance at the central chest → timer starts, three Tormentors spawn (one per conduit).
  3. Kill your Tormentor — designated player takes the final blow for Stolen Favor (only they can read the conduit's shape). Kill the Scorpius turrets that follow.
  4. Bounce energy between your plates; each catch spawns that side's Resonance. Build to x3 — never four.
  5. Favor holders call each conduit's shape: "Left needs square…"
  6. Route to whatever side spawns your needed shape (wiring crosses on floors 2–3!), then lock: shoot the Crux, stand the conductor plate as the charge arrives.
  7. All three locked → chest returns → everyone dunks (extends the timer) → kill the wave + Unstoppable Incendior → door opens.
  8. Climb and repeat — three floors total.

Map Room layout & the wiring twist

Mechanics What everything does

All three shapes now

Repository introduces the full set: Pyramidal (triangle, Left plates), Spherical (circle, Middle), and Hexahedron (square, Right). Bounces spawn the shape of the plate side that produced them. Cap x3; a fourth pickup or an expired buff kills you.

Tormentors & Stolen Favor

Three Threshold Sentinels spawn on the opening dunk, one per conduit. Killing yours unlocks your plates — and whoever lands the final blow gains Stolen Favor, the only way to read the shape your conduit demands. Assign the final blow; don't leave it to chance. Scorpius turrets spawn as the Tormentors die — kill them immediately, at range.

The bounce & the punishment

Same rhythm as Substratum: send, catch after the energy arrives, repeat. A mistimed step, a doubled plate, or an unmanned receiver breaks the chain, despawns loose Resonance, and spawns a Subjugator on your side that must die before you can retry.

Locking a conduit

Holding the matching Resonance, shoot the conduit's Crux — it starts rippling — then stand on its conductor plate as the charge enters. Because of the wiring, the shape you need may spawn on someone else's side: coordinate one last bounce, then swap sides so every conduit has a matching holder.

The clock

The Final Shape looms runs the whole floor; deposits at the reappeared chest extend it. Dunk full stacks every cycle, then clear the back wave and its Unstoppable Incendior to open the door.

Roles Three duos

Conductor player

Takes the Tormentor final blow → Stolen Favor → reads and calls the conduit shape → routes to the matching Resonance → performs the lock (Crux, then plate).

Plate partner

Anchors the bounce chain, farms stacks, shoots Scorpius on sight, and holds the side while the conductor player travels.

Everyone

x3 before the mass dunk, help burn the Incendior, and re-learn the wiring on every floor before committing.

Flow One floor (of three)

  1. Assign L/M/R duos and note this floor's wiring (see diagram).
  2. Dunk the free Resonance — timer starts, three Tormentors spawn.
  3. Kill your Tormentor (conductor player takes the last hit → Stolen Favor). On floor 2, fight it away from the ledges. Kill the turrets that follow.
  4. Bounce and farm: everyone to x3, never letting a signal land on an empty plate.
  5. Favor holders call all three conduit shapes. Plan the swap: "one more bounce, then I'm coming Right for square."
  6. Lock all three: Crux → conductor plate as the charge lands."Locked" ×3
  7. Chest returns — mass deposit, then kill the back wave + Unstoppable Incendior. Door opens; climb.

Loadout What to bring

  • Add-clear with reach: wave frames and incandescent primaries for the Dread, plus a scout/pulse/sniper that can delete Scorpius turrets across the room.
  • Unstoppable answer in every duo — an Incendior ends every floor.
  • Tormentor burst: precision heavy or a one-off super to collapse them before they close.
  • Stay mobile: nothing that wants you standing still — this whole encounter is timing and movement.
