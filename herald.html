Encounter 2 · Dissipation

Herald of Finality

First boss. Juggle plates, read the conduits, and never let a Knight touch a plate.

TL;DR The whole fight in nine lines

  1. Split into three pairs — Left, Middle, Right. Each pair owns its wing all fight.
  2. Clear your wing; kill your Trammel miniboss → one player per side gets Call to Reckoning.
  3. Reckoning players drop to the boss pit, crit the Herald's head, break the Blights that spawn, crit again → buff becomes Stolen Favor.
  4. Stolen Favor lets you read your side's conduit symbol — call it: triangle or circle.
  5. Pairs bounce the signal between their two plates; each catch spawns Resonance. Collect up to x3 — a 4th pickup kills you.
  6. Your last pickup sets your whole stack's shape — grab the called shape last.
  7. Lock all three conduits with matching Resonance → deposit chest appears center. Deposits extend the wipe timer (The Final Shape Looming, cap ~3:20).
  8. Chest expires → shield drops → group up and burn the boss.
  9. Final stand: ~30 seconds, all minibosses at once, no mechanics — dump everything.

Map Arena layout

Mechanics What everything does

The wipe timer — "The Final Shape Looming"

Always ticking down; hits zero, you wipe. Every Resonance stack deposited in the central chest adds time, capped around 3:20. The whole encounter is a race to keep feeding it.

Trammels & Call to Reckoning

Each wing hosts a Trammel, a big yellow-bar Taken miniboss. Killing it grants one player on that side Call to Reckoning — only Reckoning holders can damage the Herald's head.

Cracking the head → Stolen Favor

Reckoning players crit the head → Taken Blights spawn (one small above the boss, larger ones around the room). Destroy all Blights, crit the head again; enough damage converts the buff to Stolen Favor, which reveals the shape symbol inside your side's conduit — invisible to everyone else. Call it out.

Resonance & plate juggling

  • Two shapes appear here: Pyramidal Resonance (triangle) and Spherical Resonance (circle). No squares in this fight.
  • Stand on the lit plate to send the signal to its partner plate; a teammate must be on the receiver to catch it. Every catch spawns Resonance pickups.
  • Cap is x3. Picking up a fourth kills you instantly.
  • Your last pickup converts your whole stack to that shape — collect the called shape last.
  • A dropped catch punishes the side with a miniboss spawn and sets your conduit progress back — confirm your partner is planted before sending.

Ravenous Taken Knights

Yellow-bar Knights march at your plates in waves. If any Knight reaches a plate, the whole fireteam wipes. They outrank everything else on your screen.

Locks, deposit, damage

Lock each conduit with Resonance matching its called symbol. Three locks → the chest materializes front-center under the Witness's hand. Deposit stacks to extend the timer; when the chest vanishes the Herald's shield drops and the arena edges glow — damage phase. At low health the Herald enters a final stand: the timer resets to ~30 seconds, every Trammel spawns at once, and it's kill-or-wipe.

Roles Three pairs, one job each

Reckoning player (rotates)

Whoever gets Call to Reckoning after the Trammel dies: drop to the pit, crack the head, break Blights, earn Stolen Favor, read and call your conduit's symbol, then get back to help juggle.

Plate partner

Hold the wing solo meanwhile: Knights first, then Attendants and adds. Be ready on the receiving plate the moment your partner returns.

Everyone

Collect to x3 with the called shape last, lock or deposit as assigned, group center for DPS, and save supers + heavy for the final stand.

Flow One full cycle

  1. Encounter opens — adds flood all three wings. Clear your side; prioritize the Ravenous Knights, then kill your Trammel.
  2. One player per side gains Call to Reckoning and heads to the pit.
  3. Crit the Herald's head → Blights spawn → team breaks them all → crit again until the buff converts to Stolen Favor.Callouts: "blights up" → "blights dead" → "favor!"
  4. Stolen Favor players read their conduit and call the shape for each side: "Left circle, mid triangle, right circle."
  5. Pairs juggle plates — send, catch, collect. Build to x3 with the called shape as your final pickup.
  6. Lock all three conduits with matching Resonance.Callout: "locked" ×3
  7. Chest appears center — everyone deposits to push the wipe timer toward the ~3:20 cap.Running the At Capacity challenge? Deposit only at exactly x3.
  8. Chest expires → shield drops → group up and burn the Herald. Dodge the Witness-hand ground circles; kill exploding fliers on sight.
  9. Repeat until final stand: ~30 seconds, all Trammels up, no mechanics — commit everything to the boss.

Loadout What to bring

  • Classic strat: Well of Radiance anchor + team swords on the boss — the Herald is stationary and reachable. Any high-uptime burst (rockets with reload-swap rolls, heavy linear/sniper burst, Golden Gun) works if your team prefers range.
  • Wings are add-hell: bring real waveclear and a survivability build; there is very little cover.
  • Save it: the final stand is a 30-second DPS check — bank supers and heavy for it every cycle.
