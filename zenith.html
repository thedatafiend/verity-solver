Zenith — The Witness

Read the bracelets, pass the Tests, break six glyphs, and ride the platform up to end it.

TL;DR The whole fight in nine lines

  1. Two runners (one per side), four on add-clear. Subjugators are the only real enemy pressure.
  2. Giant hands attack each side. One hand per side wears a colored bracelet: yellow = circle, red = square, green = triangle.
  3. Runner stands in the matching attack's AoE to soak that Resonance: circle = "bees" (arm up), square = sweeping beam (arm sideways), triangle = floor pulses (arm from above).
  4. Shoot the bracelet → hand dies → you're a Glyphbreaker. Call which shape you broke — it sets the next Test.
  5. Stand in the central well of Light and shoot the matching glyph on the Witness's chest. Each break spawns Subjugators — syncing both breaks skips a set.
  6. "The Witness tests you": 14 nodes spawn mid. Shoot away the excess so the rest form the last-broken shape. Wrong shape = wipe.
  7. Six glyphs down → the platform rises — empowered DPS on the chest crit.
  8. During DPS: shift sides for the alternating sweeps, jump the shockwave (sound cue), and get off before the platform drops.
  9. Final stand at low health: a very short, all-out burn. Bank supers and heavy.

Glyphbreaker loop

Soak the matching attack until the Resonance lands, then shoot the bracelet: the hand dies and you gain Glyphbreaker. Only Glyphbreakers can damage the six chest glyphs — stand in the central well of Light and shoot the glyph matching your broken hand's symbol. Each break spawns Subjugators on both sides (two alive max); breaking both sides' glyphs simultaneously skips a spawn set.

The Test

"The Witness tests you" — a grid of 14 Resonance nodes appears front-center. Shoot away the excess nodes so what remains forms the shape of the most recently destroyed hand. Beam → square, bees → circle, pulses → triangle. The wrong final shape wipes the raid, so: one caller, everyone else trigger-discipline until the pattern is agreed.

Damage phase

All six glyphs broken → the well empowers the team and the central platform rises to the Witness — unload on the glowing chest crit. Two attacks to respect while shooting: sweeping hand AoEs that bisect the platform and alternate halves (hit lands one side → next hits the other → keep shifting), and a shockwave slam with an audio cue — jump it. When the Witness draws back and straightens, the phase is over: get off before the platform drops or die to the fall.

Final stand

At low health the Witness briefly goes immune, then reopens the crit for a very short, accelerated window. Miss it and the team wipes — arrive with supers and heavy in the tank. Aim to kill within about three damage phases.

Roles Two runners, four guns

Runners ×2 (L / R)

Read the bracelet, soak the matching attack, break the hand, call the shape ("broke triangle!"), carry Glyphbreaker to the well, break the chest glyph — synced with the other runner when possible.

Add-clear ×4 (2 per side)

Subjugators die fast, runners stay alive, and everyone helps read/shoot the Test. Machine guns earn their keep here.

Everyone

Converge for Tests and DPS. Shift for sweeps, jump the slam, leave the platform on time, save everything for final stand.

Flow One cycle

  1. Hands manifest on both sides; one per side shows its colored bracelet. Runners call it: "left green, right yellow."
  2. Runners soak the matching attack until the Resonance lands, then shoot the bracelet → Glyphbreaker.Call every break: "broke triangle" — it sets the next Test's answer.
  3. Runners hit the well and break their matching chest glyphs — simultaneously if possible. Add-clear handles the Subjugators that answer.
  4. "The Witness tests you" — everyone rotates mid, caller announces the shape, team shoots the excess nodes.Wrong shape = wipe. Caller first, bullets second.
  5. Repeat until all six glyphs are broken.
  6. Board the platform — it rises, the crit opens, burn. Shift for sweeps, jump the slam.
  7. Witness straightens → phase over → off the platform immediately. Repeat; finish in ~3 phases and save everything for final stand.

Loadout What to bring

  • Platform sustain: Well of Radiance (or strong healing support) on the rising platform is the classic anchor.
  • Burst DPS: precision heavy — snipers, linears, rockets — for the chest crit; swords are riskier here than on the Herald.
  • Machine guns for Subjugator control double as respectable backup DPS.
  • Euphony — the raid's own exotic — is a strong pick in this very room once you have it.
