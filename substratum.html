Encounter 1

Substratum

The circuit under the monolith. Two teams, eight rooms, one relentless clock.

TL;DR The whole encounter in eight lines

  1. Dunk the free Resonance in the central chest to start the wipe timer (The Final Shape looms, ~4 min). Two adjacent doors open.
  2. Split two teams of three — one pushes clockwise, one counter-clockwise. Your kills open their next door; leapfrog ~3 rooms each.
  3. Kill the Overload Minotaur in the later room → a Tormentor (Threshold Sentinel) spawns mid. All six collapse and kill it → plates activate.
  4. Bounce the pulse between paired plates: sender steps, receiver steps only when the shrinking orange circle appears. Each bounce drops Pyramidal Resonance.
  5. Collect to x3 — a 4th pickup kills you, and holding it too long kills you too. Fail the bounce on purpose after four passes so you can collect in peace — see the optimal line.
  6. Lock your conductor: holding Resonance, shoot the pillar → shoot the golden node → step the plate on the final pulse. "The resonant conductor sparks to life."
  7. Both sides locked → "You are beckoned toward the center" → kill the mid Subjugator → everyone dunks (+20 s each).
  8. Repeat three rounds — six conductors — and the way forward opens.

Optimal The failed-charge line

Same trick you'll use in Dissipation and Repository. Learn it here, where the punishment is cheapest.

Don't chain bounces into the lock. Once the plates are live, pass the signal about four times — two catches per plate — then step off and let it fail. A Subjugator spawns at the sender's room; kill it. Now every Resonance pickup you need is already on the ground, the plates are yours again, and nobody is collecting under fire.

Trying to bounce, collect, and lock in one continuous chain means someone is always doing two things at once. Splitting it into generate → clean up → collect → lock costs one scripted miniboss and removes almost every failure mode.

The line, in order

  1. Push rooms, don't clear them. Your kill opens the other trio's door — so kill the yellow-bar and call it instantly. Everything else in the room is optional; the other team is standing still until you say the word."Champ down — your door's open." Say it before you loot, reload, or regroup.
  2. Collapse hard on the Tormentor. All six, immediate, with a super or heavy if it's up. This is the one moment where six guns on one target is strictly correct, and every second here is a second off the wipe timer.
  3. Bounce four, then fail. Two catches per plate, then step off. Kill the Subjugator that answers.
  4. Collect at leisure to x3. Nothing is chasing you and nothing is on a timer but the wipe clock. Everyone walks up to exactly three — never a fourth.Remember Resonance has its own expiry: collect late, dunk promptly.
  5. Lock with the Closer already set up. Resonance in hand and crosshair on the pillar before the final pulse is sent: pillar → golden node → plate. Never improvise this sequence live.
  6. Dunk single-file. The chest takes one deposit at a time — queue up rather than crowding it, and don't leave anyone holding stacks while the next round starts.Six deposits ≈ +2 minutes. Missing two people is a round you'll pay for later.

Small things that add up

  • Fixed roles for all three rounds. Sender, Closer, add-clear — the lock sequence is muscle memory by round three only if the same hands do it every time.
  • Pre-assign the Overload answer per trio before the encounter starts. Hunting for a stun mid-room is pure lost time.
  • Don't backtrack for stragglers. Rooms are cleared to open doors, not to be safe — a dead teammate costs less than a stalled door if the timer is thin.
  • Running Scenic Route? The failed-charge window is where you sort out which Minotaur is legal to kill — do the talking during the quiet phase, not during the push.

Map The room grid

Mechanics What everything does

The clock — "The Final Shape looms"

Starts around 4 minutes when the first Resonance is dunked. Zero = wipe. Every deposit adds +20 seconds — six players dunking x3 each round is up to six minutes of extra life. The encounter is fundamentally about keeping this fed.

Rooms & doors

Two adjacent doors open per round. Each trio pushes its own direction; killing the key yellow-bar in your room opens the other team's next door, so the two teams leapfrog each other around the ring — roughly three rooms each per round. Watch doorways for exploding Supplicants.

Champions → Tormentor

An Overload Minotaur guards the later room on each side. Killing it spawns the Threshold Sentinel — a Tormentor — in the central chamber. All six collapse, burst its weak points, and drop it: "Energy flows into the architecture" — the plates in the Champion rooms light up.

The plate bounce

  • Sender steps the glowing plate → an orange pulse travels the wire to the partner plate on the other team's side.
  • A shrinking orange circle appears on the receiving plate — step on it only while the circle is visible. Too early, too late, or two people on plates at once breaks the chain.
  • Every clean bounce spawns Pyramidal Resonance at both plates and one in the middle.
  • A failed bounce spawns a Subjugator at the sender's room and locks that side's plates until it dies.

Resonance rules

Cap is x3. A fourth pickup kills you instantly, and the buff has its own timer — sitting on stacks too long also kills you. Collect late, dunk fast.

Locking a conductor

Holding Resonance, shoot the black conductor pillar — it opens to expose a golden node. Shoot the node in its short window, then step your plate as the final pulse arrives: "The resonant conductor sparks to life." Two locks per round; six total ends the encounter's objective.

The beckoning

Both conductors locked → "You are beckoned toward the center" → a Subjugator spawns mid. Kill it to activate the chest, then everyone deposits one at a time.

Roles Two trios, three jobs each

Sender

Owns the starter plate: initiates bounces, keeps the rhythm ("sent… back"), and fires the final pulse for the lock.

Closer

Far plate: steps on the circle each bounce. On the lock: Resonance in hand, pre-aimed at the pillar → node → plate. Don't improvise the timing.

Add-clear / collector

Keeps Supplicants off the plates, stuns the Overload, grabs mid-chamber Resonance, and leads the burn on Tormentor and Subjugator.

Flow One round (of three)

  1. Dunk the free Resonance at the chest — timer starts, two adjacent doors open, the Witness's hand starts pounding the center.
  2. Trios push opposite directions. Clear each room's yellow-bars to open the opposite team's next door; leapfrog ~3 rooms.
  3. Stun and kill the Overload Minotaur → Tormentor spawns mid → all six collapse and kill it."Champ down" → "Tormentor mid" → "Energy flows — plates up"
  4. Bounce the pulse — receiver steps on the circle only. After ~four passes, let it fail deliberately and kill the Subjugator that spawns.This is the whole trick: see the optimal line.
  5. Collect to x3 — never a fourth — and lock both conductors: pillar → golden node → plate on the final pulse."Sparked/locked" ×2
  6. "Beckoned" — kill the central Subjugator, then all six dunk one at a time (+20 s each).
  7. Repeat twice more. Six conductors locked and final deposits made — the encounter completes.

Loadout What to bring

  • No DPS check — bring add-clear and sustain. Wave-frame GLs, incandescent primaries, machine guns (which also stun Overloads intrinsically).
  • Overload answer per trio — a champion mod, suspend, or suppression you actually trust.
  • Burst for minibosses — a one-off super or heavy to collapse the Tormentor and Subjugators quickly in the center.
  • Healing — solar sustain or recuperation-style mods; players are frequently isolated in side rooms.
