Steps

    TL;DR The whole encounter in nine lines

    1. No boss, no DPS — three full cycles of the puzzle. Three players are teleported into solo rooms each cycle; three stay outside.
    2. Before you start: everyone wears visually distinct armor and a unique Ghost shell. This matters more than your loadout.
    3. Inside players read the three statues left to right and one of them calls the shapes: "Circle, Square, Triangle." Enter that (plus the outside statues) into the solver above.
    4. Inside: kill Knights, carry one 2D shape at a time, dunk each on the statue of the player whose statue holds that same shape. Send both of your wall shapes away — keeping one fails the hidden trade check.
    5. Outside: kill the wave (two Unstoppable Ogres), collect shapes from Knights, and run the solver's dissect order: each swap is two dissects.
    6. Mid-round the Witness kills the inside trio — scripted. Outside: dunk each Ghost at that player's matching statue pedestal. Dead players call identities from spectate.
    7. Inside, once your two needed shapes have arrived: pick up both at once → 3D aura → run through the glass wall.
    8. All three out + wave cleared → the Witness kills five of six. The survivor dunks five Ghosts at the right pedestals, fast, guided by the dead.
    9. Repeat ×3. Done.

    Map The two rooms

    Inside Playing the solo room

    1. Read and call. Find the statue wearing your armor; the shape it holds is yours. One inside player calls all three left to right — that single call powers the whole solve.
    2. The wall shows your two other shapes — these are what your Knights drop, and both must be shipped out.
    3. Pass shapes: kill a Knight, carry its shape (one at a time), and dunk it on the statue of the player whose statue holds that same shape. Out of Knights? Kill the Ogre — two more spawn.
    4. The trade rule: you must send away both of your wall shapes and receive one of each of your two needed shapes from teammates. Keeping a shape "because it's already right" fails the check even when the wall looks correct.
    5. Drop shapes before the ghost phase. Dying while holding one spawns an extra shape — seven shapes across three rooms can soft-lock the round.
    6. Escape: when your two needed shapes are in, kill two Knights and pick up both shapes at once — you gain the 3D aura (the shape not containing your own) — then sprint through the glass wall.

    Outside Dissection & Ghost duty

    1. Survive first: each wave brings two Unstoppable Ogres — stun and delete them before puzzle work.
    2. Collect components: outside Knights drop 2D shapes; Ogre kills respawn Knights when you need more.
    3. Dissect: carry a shape to a big statue whose 3D form contains it and use the Dissect prompt; do the same at a second statue and the two components swap. The solver above gives the optimal order — one caller runs it, a second player verifies each dunk before it happens.
    4. Ghost phase (mid-round): when the Witness kills the inside trio, their Ghosts appear by the six Guardian statues. Dunk each Ghost at that player's pedestal — the dead can see identities from spectate and will guide you. Wrong dunks are punished, so listen first.
    5. End of round: once all three escape and the wave is dead, the Witness kills five of six. The survivor collects and dunks five Ghosts at the right pedestals on a short timer — revived players join in as they stand up.

    Loadout What to bring

    • Unstoppable stun for every player — the Ogres come in pairs, all encounter, on both assignments.
    • Self-sufficient add-clear: Sunshot/Witherhoard-style workhorses and wave frames; inside players solo two Knights + an Ogre repeatedly.
    • Heavy for Ogres. No DPS phase exists — survivability beats spike damage.
